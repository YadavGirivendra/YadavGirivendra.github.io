01 / About

A few words
about me.

I'm a graduate researcher working at the intersection of air quality modelling, numerical modeling, and policy interventions, energy and technology transition. My current work focuses on improving reduced complexity air quality model (InMAP) and using it for helping improve the Indias air quality — playing with codes, running simulations, analysisng results, formulating hypothesis,and benchmarking them against WRF-Chem and station observations.

A typical day is split between the Go source of InMAP, Python pipelines for evaluation and visualization, and SLURM on our HPC cluster.

Beyond the technical work, I care about why this matters: India carries one of the highest particulate burdens in the world, and tools that translate emissions into exposure quickly and transparently are how we get from data to better policy.
